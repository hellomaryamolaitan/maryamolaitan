Explore a curated collection of abstract and expressive artworks, available as high-quality fine art prints.

I invite you to pause, look closer, and immerse yourself in the stories and emotions behind each piece. From the intricate layers of my abstract compositions to the tranquil expressions of my paintings, every artwork is crafted to stir memory, inspire curiousity, and offer moments of resonance.

Here, you’ll find two collections that embody different facets of my artistic journey.

The Arabirin Collection is an intimate exploration of the female form, captured through minimal strokes that highlight the elegance of curves, faces, and subtle gestures. By leaving much unsaid, these sketches invite the viewer to imagine and complete the story, celebrating the beauty and diversity of women’s bodies.

The Abstract Collection shifts into a different emotional landscape — one painted with soft yet radiant colors inspired by the calming hues of nature. These works are born out of moments of inner restlessness, offering harmony and quietness against the noise of the world.

Together, both collections are less about answers and more about invitations — to pause, to feel, and to connect with your own interpretations.

ARABIRIN

A series of minimal strokes that reveal the beauty of the female form. These portraits capture the quiet intricacies reduced to their simplest lines — curves, shoulders, and faces, inviting the viewer to complete the story. My admiration for the diversity of the woman’s body, as an artistic masterpiece in itself, led me here: its distinct lines, sizes, and subtleties, which I chose not to overstate. By leaving out details, I leave room for your imagination to step in, to feel, to see, to honour.

SOLACE

This series of abstract paintings is born from moments of seeking peace amid the chaos of self-questioning and the endless noise of the world. Using brightly subtle hues that echo the calming tones of nature, each piece creates a visual refuge — a quiet harmony for restless minds. The works are not meant to dictate meaning but to invite it. They are meditations in color and form, spaces where viewers can pause, breathe, and find their own reflection in the balance of calm and chaos.
